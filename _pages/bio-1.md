I had the 'privilege' (or maybe 'curse,' depending on who you ask) of traveling all over Turkey with my family. We moved from the far southeast to the northwest, hitting up seven different cities in Turkiye by the time I was 14. My childhood memories mostly consist of trucks and the never-ending cycle of packing and unpacking our stuff. I ranked 4th out of 50000 candidates to join [Kuleli Military High School] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuleli_Military_High_School), along with pilot training at the [Turkish Air Forces](https://hho.msu.edu.tr/Eng_default.aspx) where I had a chance to fly Cessna 172s for several sorties and completed four years of cadet training, which is where I developed the mindset I have today. ✈️
